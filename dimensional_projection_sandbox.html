Analogy 1: Line-land

How a 1D Inhabitant Sees a 2D Circle

A "Line-lander" exists purely along a 1D line axis. When a 2D Circle passes through their universe, they do not see a wheel; they only experience the physical overlap (intersection) where the circle cuts through their 1D space.

Class Challenge

Ask students: "If a 2D square passed through the line corner-first instead of a circle, how would the Line-lander's experience change?" Let them discover that the slice would expand linearly, then dramatically shift at the vertices!
