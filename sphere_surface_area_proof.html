The Visual Proof

Showing mathematically how the peeling translates to area conservation.

Step 1: Single Great Circle

A cross-section circle through the center of the sphere has radius $r$ and area $\pi r^2$.

Step 2: Peeling into 4 Circles

By slicing the sphere into 4 longitudinal segments (wedges) and flattening them, each wedge reshapes precisely into a flat circle of radius $r$.

Step 3: Total Surface Area

Since all 4 quarters combined cover the entire sphere, the total surface area must equal exactly 4 great circles.
