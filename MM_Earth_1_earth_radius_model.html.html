Geometric Model: Earth's Radius Measurement

Height dynamically adjusts via: cos(α) = r / (r + h)

Radius (r)
Tangent Line of Sight
Hypotenuse (r + h)
Standing Height (h)

Trigonometric Relationship:

In right-angled triangle ΔOAB (right-angled at A):

cos(α) = r / (r + h)  &implies;  r = (h · cos(α)) / (1 - cos(α))
