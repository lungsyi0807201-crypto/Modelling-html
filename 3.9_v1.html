Visualizing Quadratic Factorization

Welcome, Class! Today, we're going to use an Area Model to visually break down quadratic polynomials. Quadratic expressions (like $2x^2 + 5x + 3$) are hard to visualize, but they are just calculations of area!

An area model treats a multiplication (factorization) as finding the total area of a rectangle. The two linear factors on the outside are the side lengths (width and height), and the polynomial on the inside is the total area.

Try it out! Click the button to generate a new expression. Then, hover over any tile in the inner grid. Watch how it highlights the side lengths that made it, and the specific term it contributes to in the equation at the bottom.
