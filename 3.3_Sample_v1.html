Like Terms Match

A teacher-designed algebra puzzle to test your recognition of like terms.

How to Play:

  • Click two cards that contain like terms.
  • Like terms must have the exact same variables and matching exponents.
  • Watch out for variable order traps (e.g., $ab$ and $ba$ are like terms!).
  • Watch out for power traps (e.g., $x^2$ and $x$ are NOT like terms!).
