Classroom Concept: The Power of 2 Law

1. Why Odd Elimination?

Since every odd relative spot is eliminated, we strictly throw away half of the elements in each sweep. Only elements matching positions congruent to $2^k \pmod{2^{k+1}}$ survive round $k$.

2. Dynamic Index Shift

A critical teaching point: As elements vanish, the remaining items close ranks and get re-indexed starting from 1. This forces even-valued items into odd relative positions.

3. The Power of 2

Due to index resetting, the survivor is always the element that holds a position corresponding to the greatest power of 2 less than or equal to $N$.
