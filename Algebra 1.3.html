The "Border Portal" Rule

Concept: Transposition

Why does a number change signs when it crosses the Equals Sign (=)?

The Story

Imagine the = sign is an Airport Customs Border between two rival factions: Faction Plus and Faction Minus.

Under a peace treaty, characters can move back and forth, but no one is allowed to cross without a change of clothes!

When our happy +5 Gold Sun steps through the portal to the Right Side, customs officials force them to slip into a -5 Dark Cloud outfit to keep the balance!

Try it! Click the Left/Right side buttons to transport our character and observe the instant costume swap!

The "Reverse Filter"

Concept: Negative Parentheses

Why does opening parentheses change every single sign inside?

The Story

Imagine the parentheses ( ) is a cozy Camping Tent.

Outside the tent, a grumpy Reverse Filter Storm (the minus sign) is hovering, wanting to invert everyone's mood.

As long as the tent stays up, the characters are safe. But when you **take the tent down**, the storm hits *every single occupant* and flips their outfit:

  • Sunny characters (+) are soaked into rainy storms (-).
  • Rainy characters (-) find the rain funny and cheered up to sun (+).

Try it! Tap "Collapse Tent" to release the storm. Watch both characters invert.
