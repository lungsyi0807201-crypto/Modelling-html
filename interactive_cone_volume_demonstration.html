Did you know?

This formula works for all pyramids and cones! If you slice up any pyramid or cone into infinite micro-thin layers, we can use the method of exhaustion (or integration in Calculus) to prove that their volumes are always exactly $\frac{1}{3}$ of the surrounding cylinder or prism.
