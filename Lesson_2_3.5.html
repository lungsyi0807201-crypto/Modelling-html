5 Core Skills Breakdown & Action Plan

S1

Knowledge Base Structuring & RAG Chatbot Auditing

Timeline: Months 1–3 (Q1) | Target Family: Programme Officer & Content Support
Programme Officer Content / Project High AI Leverage
Why It Matters

Replaces repetitive email enquiry handling (Task 1). Shifts Alex’s role from manually answering basic course/registration questions to structuring university information systems and auditing chatbot accuracy.

Concrete Practice Activity

Take a 50-page university student handbook (PDF), re-structure it into clean Markdown/Notion metadata tables, import into a no-code RAG chatbot (e.g., Custom GPT / DocsBot AI), and execute 30 edge-case query tests.

Portfolio / Evidence Item

A hosted, public "Student FAQ Chatbot & Audit Log" containing a 3-page document detailing knowledge base schema, hallucination rates, and human escalation protocols.

How to Test Improvement
  • Achieve <5% query error/hallucination rate across 30 complex policy edge-cases.
  • Verify 100% of policy exception queries route cleanly to a human officer.
S2

AI-Assisted Differentiated Material Creation & Prompt Engineering

Timeline: Months 3–5 (Q2) | Target Family: Teaching / TA & Content Support
Teaching / TA Content / Project Pedagogical Core
Why It Matters

Accelerates lesson prep and curriculum drafting (Task 4 & 5) by 4× while maintaining HK Curriculum/CEFR standards. Prevents reliance on generic, uncurated AI output.

Concrete Practice Activity

Construct a structured System-User-Assistant prompt library in Notion to generate multi-tier DSE English reading passages, vocabulary scaffolds, and automated grading rubrics based on HK EDB frameworks.

Portfolio / Evidence Item

An online "EdTech Prompt Engineering & Scaffolding Kit" with 12 battle-tested prompt templates, input/output comparison matrix, and pedagogical validation notes for CEFR B1–C1 levels.

How to Test Improvement
  • Reduce creation time for a 3-tier differentiated lesson unit from 4 hours to <45 minutes.
  • Ensure 0 factual/grammatical errors in generated answer keys.
S3

Learning Analytics & Data Visualization Dashboarding

Timeline: Months 6–8 (Q3) | Target Family: Programme Officer & Content Support
Programme Officer Content / Project High Strategic Value
Why It Matters

Elevates administrative officers from basic record keepers to strategic data analysts (Task 12). Enables Alex to turn raw survey responses and attendance logs into visual executive reports.

Concrete Practice Activity

Clean a synthetic dataset of 500 student course evaluations using Excel (XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, Power Query), import into Google Looker Studio, and build an interactive 3-tab executive dashboard.

Portfolio / Evidence Item

An interactive, published "Programme Evaluation & Student Satisfaction Dashboard" accompanied by a 2-page executive summary outlining 3 actionable programme improvements.

How to Test Improvement
  • Extract 3 correct strategic insights (e.g., identifying risk factors for course dropout) from an unseen 200-row dataset in <30 minutes.
S4

Automated Administrative Workflow Orchestration

Timeline: Months 8–10 (Q3–Q4) | Target Family: Programme Officer & Operations
Programme Officer Content / Project Workflow Efficiency
Why It Matters

Eliminates repetitive data entry, room tracking, and receipt logging (Tasks 2, 3 & 7). Demonstrates senior-level operational capability without needing years of manual experience.

Concrete Practice Activity

Build an automated registration & feedback workflow using Zapier / Make.com connecting Google Forms, Sheets, Gmail, and Slack/Teams with conditional logic branching.

Portfolio / Evidence Item

A 3-minute video demo and architectural diagram showing an "Automated Event Management & Notification Pipeline" handling custom calendar invites, confirmations, and attendance reporting.

How to Test Improvement
  • Achieve zero-failure execution across 50 simulated user registrations with conditional logic triggers (e.g., routing special requests to staff automatically).
S5

High-Stakes Student Advisory & Exception Escalation Protocol

Timeline: Months 10–12 (Q4) | Target Family: All Job Families (Human Core)
Programme Officer Teaching / TA Content / Project Non-Automatable Core
Why It Matters

Represents the core human value that AI cannot replace (Tasks 8, 10 & 11). Focuses on crisis mentoring, academic appeal handling, and stakeholder diplomacy where empathy and policy compliance are paramount.

Concrete Practice Activity

Roleplay 10 simulated student crisis scenarios (academic plagiarism appeals, mental health distress, grade disputes) applying De-escalation Frameworks and HK University Governance Guidelines.

Portfolio / Evidence Item

A comprehensive "Student Advisory & Crisis Exception Playbook" featuring empathy scripts, regulatory decision trees, ethical AI boundaries, and referral escalation matrices.

How to Test Improvement
  • Pass a blind evaluation by a senior education administrator on a simulated crisis scenario with >85% score in empathy, policy accuracy, and escalation correctness.
