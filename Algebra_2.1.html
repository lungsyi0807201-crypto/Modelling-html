📖 Study Guide: Core Concepts

1. The Independent Variable ($x$) The distance traveled. It is "independent" because you choose the destination. It drives the change in $y$.
2. The Dependent Variable ($y$) The total fare. It depends on how far you go. This is represented by the formula outputs.
3. Linear Equation Form:
$$y = mx + c$$

$$\text{Total Fare} = (\text{Cost per km} \times \text{Distance}) + \text{Flag-fall}$$
