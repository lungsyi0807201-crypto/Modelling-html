Transport Cost Minimization Simulator

An interactive mathematical modeling matrix analyzing optimal transport modes for Hong Kong citizens traveling to Mainland China. This model minimizes total generalized travel cost: C = Price + (V × Time), where V represents the individual's subjective value of time.
