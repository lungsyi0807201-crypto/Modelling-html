Step-by-Step Refinement Logic

1

Coordinate Alignment

Set up a 2D Cartesian plane at Earth's center. The horizontal $x$-axis aligns with the Equator ($a$), while the vertical $y$-axis aligns with the rotation axis ($b$).

2

Map Real Data Points

Convert geographic measurements at latitude $\phi$ into an $(x, y)$ position. The distance $r$ to the axis provides horizontal distance $x$, while height above the equatorial plane determines $y$.

3

Isolate Polar Radius

Substitute the known equatorial radius $a$ and target coordinates $(x,y)$ into the standard centered ellipse equation to isolate and solve for the unknown polar radius $b$.

4

Model Validation

Calculate flattening $f = \frac{a-b}{a}$ to quantify deviation from a sphere, and compare against geodetic standards (e.g., WGS 84 where $f \approx 0.00335$).
