Proof of Generalization

How the co-skew factor and left dimension cancel out completely.

Let both parallelograms share the horizontal skew factor s.

We set up the coordinate system with the bottom junction at (0,0).

A = (sa - a, a)
B = (6s, 6)
C = (6, 0)

Expanding the determinant of the triangle:

det = (sa - a)(6) + 6s(-a) + 6(a - 6)
det = 6sa - 6a - 6sa + 6a - 36
det = -36

Since 6sa - 6sa = 0 and -6a + 6a = 0, the variables a and s drop out.

Theorem: The area is strictly invariant at 18 for all parallel slants and scales.
