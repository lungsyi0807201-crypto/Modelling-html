Model 1 & 3A Vulnerability

When birthday attendees dominate the group (e.g. Party 1 with 4 out of 5 celebrating), Model 1 and 3A create extreme financial leverage on minority non-birthday guests. David is penalized with 100% of the entire party bill ($2,000).

Model 2 Equitability

Model 2 enforces proportional sharing. Birthday guests still receive a substantial 50% discount off the base non-birthday rate, but non-birthday guests are protected from exponential payment spikes during multi-birthday events ($666.67 vs $2,000).

Model 3B Mutual Reciprocity

Under Model 3B, celebrants waive their own baseline cost, but still subsidize co-celebrants. This bounds David's total expense across both parties to $800.00—making it the most economical total model for David.
